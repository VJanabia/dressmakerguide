## Scoring - VERIFIED FROM OFFICIAL SCREENSHOTS (added 2026-09-21)

The site previously said no scoring system exists. That was wrong. The official Steam screenshots
show numeric attribute targets, and every value below was read by two vision models (glm-4.6v and
glm-4v-flash) at multiple crop scales, with an anti-priming control test that proved the readings are
genuine. Full claim table: docs/shot-verification.md. Scene notes: docs/shot-alt-text.json.

From shot3, the sketchbook for a garden-party commission:
- Requirements box: **Quality 70, Flowers 60, Cute 30, Romantic 45** (two models, three zoom levels, no disagreement)
- Customer budget: **800** (the number is certain; the currency glyph could not be identified)
- **"Approx. 3.85m fabric required"**
- Pattern options named on that page: Milkmaid Bodice, Double Ruffle and Bow Collar, Fae Sleeve, Single Tiered Skirt

From shot4, the fabric shop ("The International Emporium"):
- Commission limits shown as X/Y: **Quality 0/80, Formal 0/50, Simple 0/75, Professional 0/50**
- A constraint line reading **"Use only black"** with no numbers on it
- A selected bolt: **Dusty Pink Silk**, 2 metres, **Buy (100)**
- CAUTION: left = current progress, right = limit is a reading of the X/Y notation (all lefts are 0),
  not an explicit on-screen label. The models disagreed on whether a progress bar sits under each label.

From shot1, the fitting-room hand-in:
- The customer is named **Prudence**; her name card carries a **star rating** (5 stars, 1 lit / 4 unlit, above a partly filled bar)
- Her dialogue reads exactly: "Oh, how wonderful. This is MY dress, exactly as I wanted it."
- CAUTION: the presence of a star rating is solid; a finer per-star count is not defensible from a crop.

From shot6: a "SEWING COMPLETE / You may add accessories, or click below when the dress is finished."
box with a Finish Dress button, so decoration is a distinct stage after sewing.

From shot8: a "Dress Complete!" panel offering **Complete Commission**, **Keep working** and **Sell dress (869)**.

From shot7: the sewing HUD prompts read "Space Sew", "A D Rotate", "W S Change Speed".
From shot5: the fabric being cut is "Strawberry Fields Simple Cotton (10.0m)".

What is still NOT published: the currency glyph or name for coin, reputation thresholds, how the
star rating is calculated, whether targets vary by customer or by chapter, and the full attribute list.
Do not extrapolate a scoring formula from four attribute names.
